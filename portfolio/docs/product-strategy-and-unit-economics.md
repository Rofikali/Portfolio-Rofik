# Portfolio-Rofik — Product, Finance (CA/MBA) and Management Strategy

**This is a professional portfolio, not a payment product.** Financial concepts help decide whether its visibility and maintenance produce worthwhile outcomes; they do not justify premature billing, database services or user tracking.

## Business problem and job-to-be-done

- **Recruiter / hiring manager:** assess competence and evidence in under a few minutes.
- **Client / business buyer:** verify outcomes, reliability, communication and how to start a conversation.
- **Peer engineer:** inspect systems thinking, tradeoffs, real code and reproducible demonstrations.
- **Owner:** maintain a reliable public presence at low operational cost.

Primary outcome: an informed visitor can inspect real work and make contact. Success is not the number of frameworks displayed.

## Product funnel

~~~text
Discover (search/repo/profile)
    -> Understand expertise (hero, concise positioning)
    -> Evaluate evidence (projects, GitHub source, results)
    -> Build trust (tests, tradeoffs, case studies, dates)
    -> Contact / resume action
    -> Qualified conversation / opportunity
~~~

Measure each only if privacy, platform cost and owner consent are addressed. No analytics instrumentation is verified as deployed here; do not assume that a written strategy equals active measurement.

## KPIs (define, don't fabricate)

- Content freshness: last owner-confirmed review date of top project pages.
- Project evidence coverage: pages with repo link + actual implemented feature + validation proof.
- Technical quality: generated routes, broken links, WCAG findings, Core Web Vitals and check failures.
- Demand (if lawful, privacy-respecting tracking is approved): qualified inquiries / reporting period, meeting conversations, and conversions.
- Financial efficiency: cost per maintained month and owner-hours per meaningful lead, **if both are actually recorded**.

## Unit economics worksheet

| Metric | Definition | Present state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly cash operating cost | Hosting + optional domain/monitoring/analytics spend | **Not measured; no paid service approved here** |
| Maintenance opportunity cost | Hours spent × chosen hourly opportunity value | **Not measured** |
| Qualified leads | Genuine client or role conversations | **No verified tracking data** |
| Conversion rate | Qualified leads / eligible visitors | **Cannot calculate without real denominator** |
| Revenue attributable to portfolio | Confirmed contracts attributable to lead source | **Unknown; do not imply** |
| ROI | (Verified attributable benefit − full cost) / full cost | **Not estimable yet** |

Illustrative calculation only: if 10 legitimate conversations lead to 2 qualified inquiries, inquiry rate per conversation is 2/10 = 20%. This is not a site KPI or real result.

## Decisions and governance

- Prefer GitHub Pages static delivery; maintain zero unnecessary recurring services.
- Only consider paid custom domain or analytics after a small comparison of alternatives, real demand and a privacy/legal decision.
- Obtain explicit approval for a contact form, analytics cookies, user IDs, third-party embeds or collecting email details. A mailto/external contact link is different from hosting personal information.
- Portfolio case studies may document actual shops or software projects, but **do not reveal customer financial data, private credentials or unapproved testimonials**.
- Review public resume experience dates and skill percentages with the owner; replace inflated-looking percentages with evidence-based proficiency descriptions if appropriate.

## Risks and anti-goals

Over-engineering, false "production" claims, stale skills, broken demos, uncontrolled costs, vanity metrics and poor mobile accessibility can undermine trust more than missing new features. A small and accurate portfolio can beat a large unverified dashboard.

## Product roadmap gate

Before accepting a proposed feature, answer: Who benefits? What evidence demonstrates need? What is smallest static alternative? What are security/a11y/SEO and recurring costs? How will success be observed without violating privacy? What should be removed if this is added?
